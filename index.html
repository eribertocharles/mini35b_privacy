Privacy Policy — mini 35B

Last updated: 13 November 2026

mini 35B is an AI chat app whose model runs on your iPhone. In short: mini 35B does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Your conversations are processed and stored on your iPhone, and the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What mini 35B does with your conversations

  • Your chats stay on your device. Each chat is saved locally, in the app's private storage on your iPhone, so your history is there when you come back. A saved chat holds your messages, the model's replies and reasoning, the web pages and charts it made, the steps it took with its tools, any summary it wrote to keep a long chat going, timing figures for each reply, and the chat's title or the name you gave it.
  • The model's memory of a chat stays on your device too. For your three most recent chats, the app keeps the model's working memory of the conversation in its cache folder so a chat can pick up where it left off. It skips this when your iPhone is short on space, and iOS may clear the cache at any time; the chat itself is unaffected. While it answers, the app also uses short-lived working files that it removes automatically.
  • Nothing is uploaded. mini 35B does not send your messages, replies, pages, or any conversation content anywhere. There is no server to send them to.
  • You decide how long chats are kept. There is no time limit: a chat stays until you delete it. Long-press a chat in the chat list and choose Delete Chat; this removes the chat and the model's saved memory of it. Deleting mini 35B from your iPhone removes all of its chats and settings. Copies in iCloud or computer backups made before you deleted something stay in those backups until you delete or replace them.

Sharing and copying

Nothing leaves the app unless you send it. Export as Markdown in a chat's long-press menu writes the chat to a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet; it goes only where you choose, such as Files, Notes, or AirDrop. The app clears its copy of exported files the next time it starts. Copy buttons put a reply, code, or a chart on the clipboard only when you tap them, and the app never reads your clipboard.

You can also select text in a chat and use iOS's own menu (Copy, Share, Translate, Look Up, Search Web). Those are iOS features: anything you send through them is handled by Apple or by the service you pick, under their privacy practices.

Network use

mini 35B makes no network connections of its own, with one exception: the AI model itself, about 15 GB, which is too large to ship inside the app. It is delivered by Apple, from Apple's servers, using Apple's Background Assets system. The App Store downloads it when you install the app, and iOS continues the download while the app is closed. If you remove the model in Settings, or a download fails, the app asks iOS to fetch it again when you tap download. Apple's servers see that request as they would any App Store download, and Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy. We receive no information from it, and no server run by us or by anyone other than Apple is involved.

This is a transfer to your device. No messages, replies, or other personal information are ever transmitted from your device by mini 35B. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services, no update check, and no account to sign in to. Once the model is on your iPhone, chatting needs no internet connection.

If a reply contains a link and you tap it, iOS opens it in your browser or the app registered for that link. mini 35B fetches nothing itself, and nothing is opened unless you tap. The website you visit then follows its own privacy practices.

Web pages the model writes

When you ask for a web page, it runs in a sealed preview with no internet access. The libraries and fonts that pages commonly link to (such as Three.js, Chart.js, and Google Fonts) are served from copies inside the app, so no outside server is contacted. Anything a page stores is kept only in memory while its preview is open and is discarded when the preview closes; links inside it do not open, and it cannot reach the rest of the app. To catch mistakes, the app also runs each page it writes in a hidden copy of the same preview and tells the model about any errors; that check stays on your iPhone. The model's tools, such as editing a page or drawing a chart, work inside the app; none of them opens a website, reads your files, or uses the network.

Permissions

mini 35B asks for no permissions. It does not request access to your camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts, or calendars, and it sends no notifications.

The AI model

The model runs on your iPhone's own hardware. Your prompts are processed on the device and are never sent to us, to the model's original authors, or to any outside service.

Because the model runs locally and is not connected to any live source, its answers can be wrong or out of date. Please don't rely on it for medical, legal, or financial decisions.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-up, no login, and no account. mini 35B does not use the Advertising Identifier, does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The open-source libraries it uses are listed, with their licenses, in Settings > About > Acknowledgements.

Data storage and security

Besides your chats, mini 35B stores a few settings on your iPhone: whether generation stats are shown, the size of its model cache, and memory and loading-time measurements it takes on your phone to size that cache. None of these hold chat content or anything that identifies you. iOS also keeps the message you were typing and the chat on screen, so they come back if iOS closes the app in the background.

All of this lives in the app's private, sandboxed storage, protected by iOS's standard data protection. The app does not sync anything through iCloud and keeps nothing on any server. Like most app data, your chats and settings are included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups; the model's saved memory and temporary files are not. Any diagnostic messages the app writes go to your iPhone's own system log, contain no chat text, and are not sent anywhere by the app.

Delete Model in Settings removes the downloaded model and keeps your chats.

Children's privacy

mini 35B collects no personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all. It runs a general-purpose AI model.
